## 1. Introduction for Travelers
Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (formerly Aurangabad) is globally renowned as the heritage gateway to the UNESCO World Heritage **Ajanta Caves** (dating back to the 2nd century BCE) and **Ellora Caves** (home to the monolithic Kailasa Temple).

Exploring these rock-cut caves involves extensive walking, hill climbing, and exposure to intense dry heat, where midday temperatures regularly exceed 38°C to 42°C (100°F to 108°F) between March and June. 

Maintaining electrolyte hydration is critical for travelers. While bottled mineral water is essential, fresh fruit and sugarcane juices provide immediate cellular hydration, natural potassium, and fatigue relief. This guide equips visitors with essential tips for safely enjoying India's legendary fresh juice culture without compromising digestive health.

## 2. The Traveler's 5 Safety Rules for Fresh Juice in India
When ordering fresh beverages in India, following these simple guidelines ensures a safe, rejuvenating experience:

1. **Insist on 'No Added Ice' (Or Verify RO Purified Ice):**
   * *The Risk:* Street vendors often purchase commercial block ice that is transported without sanitation wrapping and is meant only for cooling crates, not consumption.
   * *Best Practice:* Request *"Bina baraf"* (Without ice) or visit established juice centers that manufacture in-house Reverse Osmosis (RO) ice cubes.
2. **Watch the Pressing Live:**
   * Avoid juice kept in open glass pitchers or pre-poured plastic jugs. Always patronize centers where the fruit or cane is pressed fresh directly into your glass upon ordering.
3. **Opt for Thick-Skinned Fruits:**
   * Fruits with natural protective rinds—such as Mosambi (sweet lime), Santra (mandarin orange), Pomegranates, and Pineapples—are naturally shielded from airborne contaminants prior to peeling.
4. **Choose Establishments with Stainless Steel Machinery:**
   * Verify that the extraction rollers are food-grade stainless steel rather than unshielded cast iron.
5. **Inspect Citrus Slicing Surfaces:**
   * The cutting board and knives must be clean and sanitized regularly with potable water.
